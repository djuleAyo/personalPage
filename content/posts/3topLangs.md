Tree languages are often portrayed as pinnacle of programming capability

lisp for its homoiconicity and macros
prolog for its declarative nature and inference engine
haskell for its purity and type system

These programming languages pioneered concepts and largely influenced many.
Prolog and Lisp are considered as native for AI programming.
Yet amount of job ads for these converges to zero. The question is
how come source of inspiration and innovation is not in wide use?
How come their supperior design didnt prosper in the market?

TBH I dont have definite answers. Yet I did spend a while working in these languages
and I do immensely value declarative paradigm so here is my take.

1. Linguistic relativity

Let me set the stage real quick.
That linguistic relativity thing is real. Programmers can have direct experience
of how language shapes thinking. Much more so that anybody else.
Transitioning from anything to prolog or haskell is exactly discovery of that impact.
You change from imperative to declarative thinking. From thinking in terms of
how to build something to what it is. Naming it what it is correctly brings
it to existence. How its build is delegated to the language engine.

2. Escalation

Properties of learning a new language, be it programming or natural are reoccurring.
At first you learn letters - atoms of language. Unbreakable. Then you learn words,
then you learn how to hear others speak and finally you learn to express yourself.
Each stage is an escalation. And dramatic one. While learning new letter system
not the same as your native one it hard to imagine how could you even combine
those letters into words, let alone hearing and speaking. Yet those escalations
do happen in time. You can end up being expressive. Even artistically so.
That escalation lets you leap worlds of difference in comparison to learning stages.
And once escalation happens it rises new insights as in point 1.
So lets give an example. Note is an atom. Chord is a word. Perfect hearing is
language of those words. One say you cant learn perfect hearing its genetics.
Others and me claim you can. There is no structural difference in escalation
between letter word speaking and note chord music. One thing is missing.

3. Plasticity

Kids learn languages fast. It seems formulae to fast learning is proper combination
of emotional engagement and structure. Beautiful. You need both hemispheres engaged
and cooperating for knowledge. Exactly the ancient teaching. Kids feeling everything
intensely does help in the process. Once you are in "ive seen it all" state,
its hard to get interested let alone learn anything. You comprehend the structure
just for it to evaporate. So structure, drama, sleep is the formulae.
Never skip learning about personal lifes of great programmers/mathematicians etc
it will give you the exact emotional engagement you need for learning.

Now with some important references to language learning and relation of language
and cognition we can slowly circle back to our original question.
what about our 3 pivots? If point of language is escalation and expressing one
self why most expressive languages are not in wide use?

* optimizing for machine
* optimizing for fragmentation - resell same thing in different packages
* optimizing for short term gains - quick to learn, quick to deliver
* optimizing for popularity - follow trends and easy 'hello world' examples

We rebuild same things over and over again which is exactly what automation is not.
